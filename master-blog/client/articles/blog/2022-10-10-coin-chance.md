An interesting discussion with roommates:

> If you flip a coin 1,000,000 times, and you obtain tails only 20 times, then your results imply that the coin must not be a fair coin.

Let's translate the question to a more meaningful form: chance of obtaining tails $\in \{ 0, 1, ..., 19 \}$, which means we have to prove that the number of flipping the coin required for obtaining tails is lower than 20.

Assume that the coin is a fair coin, we have $\frac{1}{2}$ chance to get a tail every time we flip it. For 20 times of obtaining tails, the possibility is:

$$
\frac{1}{2^{20}} \times 1000000
$$

$$
\log_2^{1000000} = 19.9315
$$
