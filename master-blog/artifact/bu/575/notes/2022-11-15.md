### Deal with Page Faults

Use algorithms:

- **Frame-Allocation**
- **Page-Replacement**

Shrink the world. Always start from top to bottom (FIFO).

Page Fault is a machism to bring new pages in when there is no enough pages (pages with invalid bit) for the comming frames.

- **Invalid Page**: Pages not occupied
- **Valid Page**: Pages occupied by frames
