I was confident about my career future back to May when the time I just arrived the states. However, the situation was only getting worse. Everything in tech seemed to be collapsing, companies such as Amazon, Meta, and Google announced that the layoff would last in next few months, and other companies retrieved their job posts on websites without presence. Even people already received return offers from Big Techs for next year have been rescinded. I was like:

- `08/22`: Big Techs should come to me
- `09/22`: Medium companies sound good
- `10/22`: There are career fairs! I should attend it
- `11/22`: I got a few interviews from famous firms. Maybe I shouldn't have been that worried
- `12/22`: Everything is doomed. Let's spend some time on the final exam
- `01/23`: Please give me a chance to work
