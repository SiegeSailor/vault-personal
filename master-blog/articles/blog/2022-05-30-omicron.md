This happened in the first week I arrived the states. Although I count my days only when I start feeling bad, I had already been easy-tired 3-4 days before:

1. **Day 1**: Shiver and dizzy.
2. **Day 2**: Sore throat and painful, sputum, dizzy, shiver, and tired.
3. **Day 3**: Sore throat and painful, more sputum, cough, less dizzy, less shiver, tired, runny nose, stuffy nose, Headache and muscle aches.
