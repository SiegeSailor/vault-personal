# Introduction

> This document is for Full Stack Development. Including my work experience in Edallianz as a **UIUX Specialist and Associate Front-End Engineer** and as a **Full Stack Engineer**.

## Origin

Before stepping into the programming world, I was working as a **Graphic Designer** and **Marketing Person**, my job was to promote medical machines and educate new medical theories to patients and doctors. In the 17 months, I had to create a variety of media with my **Branding** and **Marketing** knowledge, establish the design guideline for the department.

In the process, I gradually found out that my design and promoting direction was basic on the audience, we call **User Experience** in the other way, and the way I create layouts of media collateral was like how people think about **User Interface**. Also, I have been curious about the way how the user flow is in games, apps, websites, and anything people use in daily life.

That was why I changed my career path. I am working as a **UI/UX Specialist and Associate Front-End Engineer** now, everything in this field is interesting but new for me, I want to write all of them down into this GitBook.

Let's draw a line here, now is 20190813, all my coworkers has been disemployed except for me, and I am surviving as a **Full Stack Engineer**. I am leaving soon as there is no much reasonable decision have been made in this company, there is no reason for me to stay with such pay and direction, I will leave after learning all I want.
