%=======================================================================
| Question 1: Convert binary 100101111010 to octal and hexadecimal     |
%=======================================================================

# Octal

First of all, write 100101111010 in the following format:
100 101 111 010

Then calculate every 3 digits as octal, so we will get:
4 5 7 2

Answer: 4572

# Hexadecimal

Write 100101111010 in the following format:
1001 0111 1010

Then calculate every 4 digits as hexadecimal, so we will get:
9 7 A

Answer: 97A

%=======================================================================
| Question 3: Convert binary 1011110 to decimal                        |
%=======================================================================

1011110 is:
1 \times 2^6 + 0 \times 2^5 + 1 \times 2^4 + 1 \times 2^3 + 1 \times 2^2 + 1 \times 2^1 + 0 \times 2^0
64 + 0 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 + 0
= 94

Answer: 94

%=======================================================================
| Question 4: Convert hexadecimal FFFF to decimal                      |
%=======================================================================

FFFF is:
15 \times 16^3 + 15 \times 16^2 + 15 \times 16^1 + 15 \times 16^0
61440 + 3840 + 240 + 15
= 65535

Answer: 65535
