### Reflexive

$$
y \sim x
$$

$$
y - x = 0, m | 0 \\
\forall m \ y - x = m \times k, k = 0
$$

### Symmetric

$$
x \sim y \implies y \sim x
$$

$$
x - y = k \times m \implies y - x = -k \times m
$$

### Transitive

$$
x \sim y \land y \sim z \implies x \sim z \\
x \sim y \implies x - y = k_1 \times m, x = k_1 \times m + y \\
y \sim z \implies y - z = k_2 \times m, z = y - k_2 \times m
$$

$$
x - z = (k_1 \times m + y) - (y - k_2 \times m) = (k_1 + k_2) \times m
$$

### Euler Theorem Table

| $z^x_7$ | $g^1$ | $g^2$ | $g^3$ | $g^4$ | $g^5$ | $g^{6 = 0}$ |
| ------- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----------- |
| $1$     | $1$   | $1$   | $1$   | $1$   | $1$   | $1$         |
| $2$     | $2$   | $4$   | $1$   | $2$   | $4$   | $1$         |
| $3$     | $3$   | $2$   | $6$   | $4$   | $5$   | $1$         |
| $4$     | $4$   | $2$   | $1$   | $4$   | $2$   | $1$         |
| $5$     | $5$   | $4$   | $6$   | $2$   | $3$   | $1$         |
| $6$     | $6$   | $1$   | $6$   | $1$   | $6$   | $1$         |

- $| 1 |$ = $1$
- $| 2 |$ = $3$
- $| 3 |$ = $z^x_7$

#### Explanation

For $z^x_7$ with $1$, $1^1, 1^2, 1^3, ..., 1^6$ are all $1$ and will always get `1` after modularizing by `7`. $| 1 |$ is `1` is because there is only `1` kind of number in the group.

We can also do $g^1, g^2, ..., g^8$ for x-axis and $1, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29$ for y-axis with $z^x_{30}$.
