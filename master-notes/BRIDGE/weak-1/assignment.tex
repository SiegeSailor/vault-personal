Consider the following pieces of identification a person might have in order to apply for a credit card:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[B:] Applicant presents a birth certificate.
    \item[D:] Applicant presents a driver's license.
    \item[M:] Applicant presents a marriage license.
\end{enumerate}

Write a logical expression for the requirements under the following conditions:

\begin{quote}
    \textbf{b. }The applicant must present at least two of the following forms of identification: birth certificate, driver's license, marriage license.

    \(\textbf{Answer:}\enspace(B \land D) \lor (B \land M) \lor (M \lor D)\)
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
    \textbf{c. }Applicant must present either a birth certificate or both a driver's license and a marriage license.

    \(\textbf{Answer:}\enspace B \lor (D \land M)\)
\end{quote}

Express each of the following English sentences with a logical expression:

    \textbf{e. }Being able to park in the school parking lot implies that the person is either a senior or at least 17 years old.

    \(\textbf{Answer:}\enspace p \implies (s \lor y)\)
\end{quote}
