I started my MSCS at Boston University in Summer 2022 as an international student. As non-local, there are 2 things must be considered with full priority:

1. Tuition and Living Fees
2. Career Path

The timeline for studying Master's degree offered by BU MET CS is flexible. You can choose to complete it in 1 year without internship, or in 2 years with 2 period of internship.
